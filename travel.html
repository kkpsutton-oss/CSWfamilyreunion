Travel & Lodging

Everything you need to plan your trip to Atlanta, GA — flights, driving, hotels, and rentals.

📍 Reunion Location: Atlanta, Georgia • July 14–18, 2027. Exact venue address will be shared closer to the date — but go ahead and book your travel for the Atlanta area now!
💡 Pro tip: Book early! July is peak travel season. We recommend booking flights and hotels at least 3–4 months in advance to get the best rates.

🛏️ Planning the Drive

At ~14–15 hours, most families split this into two days with an overnight stop — popular options include the Knoxville, TN or Charlotte, NC areas, roughly halfway along the route. Plan to arrive a day before the reunion kicks off on July 14 to allow buffer time for traffic and rest stops.
